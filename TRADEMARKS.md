# Trademarks and branding

The names **Tinlance**, **Tinlance Limited**, **TLMS**, associated logos and product marks are identifiers of their respective owners. No trademark licence is granted by access to this public repository.

Customers receiving white-label rights must follow the branding and attribution terms in their executed agreement. A white-label agreement must state which marks may be removed, which legal notices must remain, and whether customer modifications may be represented as Tinlance products.

Before launch, conduct trademark clearance in target markets and document ownership, registrations, permitted uses and enforcement contacts.

This notice is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific trademark advice.
